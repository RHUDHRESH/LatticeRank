## Top three by expected damage

1. **R2 (signature leak)** — invalidates the current evidence base. Everything else is
   measured *through* this distortion, so it must be neutralised first or every subsequent
   number is uninterpretable.
2. **R1 (periodic aliasing)** — the actual competition problem, pre-announced by the
   organiser as the decisive case, and confirmed here as 100% of catastrophic failures.
3. **R3 + R4 (domain shift both ways)** — DriftForge is simultaneously *narrower* than the
   sponsor structurally and *broader* photometrically. Neither generator alone is a safe
   proxy for the hidden test; validation must span both.
